# Inject 9 — A complaint

**Monday 23 November. Email from the Head of Patient Safety, Kingsmere, to Wardline**

"We are investigating a complaint. On Friday 2 October an 87-year-old care home resident was discharged from Ward 9 and arrived at her care home by hospital transport at 22:51. The home would not admit her at that hour. She was brought back and spent another night with us. The incident reference is INC-2026-13344.

Her daughter's complaint says a nurse told her 'they need the beds for the weekend, the new system says it will be very busy'.

Our records show 103 discharges that Friday against a typical 90 or so. I understand your forecast showed the Saturday as 'red' on each of the three preceding days, and that the Saturday was in fact quiet.

Please send: the forecasts displayed for 3 October on 30 September, 1 October and 2 October; a list of the staff roles that viewed them; and your view on whether the forecast contributed."
