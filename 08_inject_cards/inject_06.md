# Inject 6 — Kenya: a deadline and a letter

**Wednesday 18 November. Two messages.**

**From Daniel Mwangi, Afya Bora Foundation:** "Our board meets on 2 December. To include Kisima I need the signed term sheet by 30 November. One addition from our data team: patient-level admissions including diagnosis, monthly, to our regional data platform. This is now standard for our grantees."

**From Dr Achieng Odhiambo, Medical Superintendent, Kisima County Referral Hospital:** "Colleagues, I hear the Foundation wishes to move quickly. I will be direct. We have given you our registers and our time. My staff ask me what they will receive. The nurses' union has today issued a fourteen-day strike notice in this county over unpaid allowances. If you are coming, come knowing this, and stay. If you cannot stay, tell me now and I will not think less of you. What I cannot accept is another dashboard and another goodbye."
