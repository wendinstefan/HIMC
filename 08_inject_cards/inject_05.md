# Inject 5 — The investor's terms in writing

**Tuesday 17 November. Email from Tobias Kern, Northlight Ventures, to the board and leadership group**

"Following the board discussion, our investment committee has approved the following, subject to documents.

**Option A — £1.5m bridge**, convertible, at a 30% discount to the next round. Conditions: (1) a signed, paid Horizon pilot with an NHS trust, announced publicly, by 31 December 2026; (2) the announcement to include a headline accuracy figure 'at least comparable to competitors'; (3) a second reference site contracted by 31 March 2027.

**Option B — £0.6m**, same instrument, no Horizon conditions. Condition: monthly net outflow reduced to £170,000 by 1 February 2027.

We need to know which by Friday 27 November. For what it is worth, the committee preferred A."
