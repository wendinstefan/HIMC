# Inject 8 — A journalist has the sales deck

**Friday 20 November, 11:20. Email to the Chief Executive, forwarded to the leadership group**

"I'm a reporter at *Health Systems Weekly*. I've been sent a Wardline document headed 'Horizon sales deck — claims page' which says the product is 'up to 98% accurate', saves '£3.2 million per hospital, every year' and is 'clinically safe' and 'trusted by front-line staff'. I've also seen a letter from staff representatives at an NHS trust saying a forecast from your system was used to move a nurse and was wrong.

1. Do you stand by the 98% and £3.2m figures?
2. Has Horizon been clinically validated? By whom?
3. Is patient data used to train it anonymous?
4. Were staff at the trust told they were part of a trial?
5. Is Horizon a medical device?

I'm filing at 5 p.m. I have also asked the trust, under the Freedom of Information Act, for the impact assessment and any accuracy reports."
