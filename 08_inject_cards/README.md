# Inject cards

Things happen while you work. These ten cards are emails, letters and messages that arrive at Wardline between **9 and 24 November 2026**, after your work starts on Friday 6 November.

The brief warns you: *"Additional stakeholder perspectives or unexpected challenges may emerge as the project progresses."* These are they.

## How to use them

- **Open one card at a time, in number order. Do not read ahead.** The point is to respond to something you did not see coming, as a real leadership team would.
- **Give each card 10 – 15 minutes.** Decide what you would do, then carry on with your main work.
- **Check what the card claims.** People in these messages are under pressure and some of them are wrong. Most cards can be checked against the files in folders `01` – `07`, and some bring new figures you do not have elsewhere.
- **Let it change your recommendation, or say why it does not.**
- If your session leader is releasing the cards, follow their timing instead.
