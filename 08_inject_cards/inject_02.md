# Inject 2 — The Caldicott Guardian's ruling

**Tuesday 10 November. Letter from Dr Imran Sheikh, Caldicott Guardian, and Ruth Castellanos, Data Protection Officer, Kingsmere**

"We have completed our review of the extract supplied to Wardline in June.

1. The national data opt-out should have been applied. It was not. Records for patients who had opted out, about 5% of the total, must be removed.
2. We will supply a replacement extract. Unless Wardline gives us, within five working days, a specific and documented purpose for each of ethnic category, deprivation decile and postcode district, the replacement will not contain them.
3. All copies of the June extract must be deleted within ten working days, with written confirmation of every location it was held.
4. No forecast derived from trust data may be shown to staff for operational use until a new agreement and a jointly signed impact assessment are in place. A shadow pilot from January remains possible.

We note that your own team has recorded that the current model does not use patient-level data at all."
