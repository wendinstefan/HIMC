# Inject 4 — The fortnight

**Monday 16 November. Email from Tom Hallworth, Deputy Chief Operating Officer, Kingsmere**

"You will have seen the news. Ashby Vale's emergency department was shut from Monday to Friday last week after a burst water main, and everything came to us. We also lost Ward 5 to norovirus from the 5th.

I looked at Horizon every morning.

| | Your forecast the day before (occupied adult beds) | Beds occupied | Beds open | Waiting in the emergency department for a bed, 08:00 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mon 9 | 451 | 450 | 451 | 32 |
| Wed 11 | 454 | 449 | 449 | 28 |
| Fri 13 | 458 | 445 | 446 | 38 |
| Sat 14 | 450 | 468 | 468 | 47 |

On paper you were right: within a few beds on most days. Your people tell me the error for the fortnight was about two per cent, better than in testing. I believe them. That is what worries me.

On Friday you forecast 458 occupied beds. I had 446 beds open. Ward 5 was shut and your system did not know. And on no day did it tell me the one thing I needed, which is how many I was short. On Saturday I had forty-seven people waiting for beds, fifty-five who had waited over twelve hours, every escalation bed open and the hospital at one hundred per cent. Your screen was red all week. So was the view from my window.

I am the person who has been arguing for you in this building. Tell me what I say to my Chief Operating Officer. And tell me honestly whether this is fixable by Christmas."
