# Inject 3 — Letter from the staff-side chair

**Wednesday 11 November. Letter to the Chief Nurse, copied to Wardline**

"On behalf of the joint staff side I am writing about the 'Horizon' forecasting trial.

Our members were not told that a trial was taking place, nor that a log was being kept of which staff looked at the system and when. On 1 October a forecast from this system was used to justify moving a registered nurse from her ward. The forecast was wrong. Her ward worked short. She was told 'the computer says Saturday will be red'.

Before any further use we ask for three things in writing:

1. A statement, agreed with us, of what this tool will **never** be used for. We propose: decisions about individual patients' discharge; reductions in planned staffing; performance management of individuals.
2. Staff-side representation on whatever group oversees it, and a simple way for any member of staff to report that it was wrong.
3. That night staff and healthcare assistants are asked what they think. They were not.

If we do not receive these we will advise members not to use the system and not to act on it."
