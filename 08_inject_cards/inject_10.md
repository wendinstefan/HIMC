# Inject 10 — The Clinical Safety Officer will not sign

**Tuesday 24 November. Email from Dr Niamh Brennan, contract Clinical Safety Officer**

"I have read the model card and the trial notes.

I will not sign a safety case for Horizon as described. The stated intended use includes 'discharge planning'. If the product is presented as informing decisions about when individual patients leave hospital, I believe it would need to be assessed as a medical device, and that is a different project lasting many months. If the intended use is limited to site-level capacity planning, with individual patient decisions explicitly excluded, a safety case is achievable. I estimate thirty days of my time. I work two days a week and my contract ends on 31 January.

For the hazard log I need answers to: who is the named person who withdraws the forecast when it is wrong; how users are told the data is stale; who reads the overrides; and what the hospital does on a day when the forecast is unavailable. At present the answer to all four is 'nobody' or 'nothing'.

I would also want the 'beds needed' title removed from a number that is not beds needed."
