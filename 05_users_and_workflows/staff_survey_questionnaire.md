# Staff survey — questionnaire and method

Results are in `staff_survey_responses.csv` (412 responses).

## Method

| | Kingsmere (UK) | Kisima (Kenya) | Sukamaju (Indonesia) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Responses | 262 | 68 | 82 |
| How it was distributed | Link on the intranet; email forwarded by divisional leads; QR code posters in staff rooms | Paper forms handed out at two daytime nursing meetings and keyed in by a research assistant | Online form shared in staff WhatsApp groups by the deputy director |
| Language | English | English | Bahasa Indonesia (answers translated into English by an agency) |
| Staff who could have answered | About 3,900 clinical and operational staff | About 300 | About 620 |

- The survey was open from 6 July to 2 October 2026.
- It was anonymous. Role, years in role and shift pattern were self-reported.
- No incentive was offered.
- Questions 7 to 13 were on a second page. Thirty-eight respondents stopped after the first page; their later answers are blank.
- Nobody was asked whether they had seen the prototype. Most had not.

## Questions

**About you**

- Site
- Role (list)
- Years in role: less than 1 / 1–3 / 4–9 / 10 or more
- Usual shift pattern: days / nights / rotating

**Q1.** How confident are you in predicting how full your ward or hospital will be **24 hours** from now? (1 = not at all, 5 = very)

**Q2.** How confident are you in predicting how full it will be **7 days** from now? (1–5)

**Q3.** In a typical shift, about how many hours do you spend on bed management: finding beds, chasing discharges, reporting numbers? (hours)

**Q4.** Which of these do you use now to know the bed position? (tick all that apply)

**Q5.** "A forecast of bed demand for the next seven days would be useful in my job." (1 = strongly disagree, 5 = strongly agree)

**Q6.** "I would trust a forecast from an algorithm as much as one from an experienced colleague." (1–5)

**Q7.** If the forecast and your own judgement disagreed, which would you follow? (The forecast / My own judgement / Escalate to someone senior / Depends who is accountable)

**Q8.** How would you prefer a forecast to be shown? (Single number / Number with a range / Best case, expected, worst case / Red, amber, green only)

**Q9.** Which decision would it help most? (list, including "Nothing — my decisions are made too early or too late for a 7-day view")

**Q10.** What concerns you about a tool like this? (tick all that apply)

**Q11.** What device do you mainly use at work? (list)

**Q12.** How many dashboards or regular reports are you already expected to look at? (number)

**Q13.** Anything else you want us to know? (free text)
