# Wardline Health — investor update, third quarter 2026

**Sent by Freya Lindholm to shareholders, 16 October 2026.**

Dear investors,

A busy quarter. The headlines:

## Highlights

- **Horizon is working.** Our seven-day bed-forecasting prototype is achieving **97%+ accuracy** in back-testing on three and a half years of data from a large NHS teaching hospital.
- **Strong engagement in the Kingsmere trial.** 27 front-line and operational staff have used Horizon since 7 September, with hundreds of sessions logged.
- **A growing pipeline.** £4.3m of annual contract value across 28 open opportunities in eight countries.
- **International pull.** Hospitals in Kenya and Indonesia have approached us and shared data. A foundation has offered USD 150,000 to fund a Kenyan pilot.
- **Customer base stable** at eight hospitals, with our first customer outside the UK live in Ireland.

## Numbers

| | Q3 2026 |
|---|---|
| Annual recurring revenue | £1.49m |
| Cash at 30 September | £2.39m |
| Average monthly net burn | £204k |
| Headcount | 36 |

## Lowlights

- Recurring revenue is flat on the previous quarter.
- NHS procurement remains slow: our median sales cycle for won deals is about 17 months.
- A competitor has announced a forecasting pilot.

## Asks

- Introductions to chief operating officers and chief finance officers at NHS acute trusts.
- Introductions to funders interested in digital health in East Africa and South-East Asia.

## Next quarter

- Sign the first paid Horizon pilot.
- Publish Horizon accuracy results.
- Decide on international expansion.

Thank you for your continued support.

Freya
