**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE — London, 14 October 2026**

## Claros Flow and Pennine Vale Hospitals NHS Trust to put AI bed forecasting live before Christmas

Claros Flow, the AI command-centre company, today announced a partnership with Pennine Vale Hospitals NHS Trust to predict bed demand across the trust's two hospitals up to seven days ahead.

The Claros Flow forecasting engine delivers **94% accurate seven-day bed forecasts**, giving operational leaders the confidence to plan staffing, protect planned surgery and get ahead of winter pressure.

"For too long hospitals have managed beds by looking in the rear-view mirror," said Marcus Thorne, Chief Executive of Claros Flow. "Our AI turns the lights on. Trusts using Claros Flow know on Monday what Friday looks like."

Helen Ashby, Chief Operating Officer at Pennine Vale Hospitals NHS Trust, said: "We are looking forward to working with Claros Flow this winter and to seeing what the technology can do for our patients and staff."

The pilot will go live before Christmas. Claros Flow will publish results in spring 2027.

**About Claros Flow.** Claros Flow builds AI-powered command centres for health systems. The company has raised £38 million and works with NHS trusts and integrated care boards across England.
