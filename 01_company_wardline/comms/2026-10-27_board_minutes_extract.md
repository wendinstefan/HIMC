# Wardline Health Ltd — board meeting, 27 October 2026

**Extract of minutes: items 4 to 7. Confidential.**

Present: Margaret Oyelaran (Chair), Freya Lindholm (CEO), Dev Ramanathan (CTO), Callum Whitby (CFO), Tobias Kern (Northlight Ventures), one independent director. In attendance for item 5: the Horizon leadership group.

## 4. Finance

- CW presented management accounts to 30 September. Cash £2.39m. Net outflow averaged £204k a month in the third quarter.
- The innovation grant (£23.3k a month) ended on 30 September. October's outflow will be higher.
- Recurring revenue has been flat since the Aire & Wharfe contract ended in April. One new Wardline Live contract has been signed in 2026 (Slaney Valley University Hospital, Ireland, about £50k a year). No NHS trust has signed since December 2025.
- CW presented the base-case plan: four Horizon hires between January and March 2027; first Horizon revenue in February 2027; Horizon recurring revenue of about £105k a month by October 2027. **On this plan cash is exhausted in June 2027.**
- TK noted that the plan needs roughly £1.25m of new annual Horizon revenue within twelve months from a product that has no customers, and asked what the weighted pipeline is. CW: about £540k of annual contract value, weighted by the sales team's own probabilities.
- **Agreed:** CW to bring a downside case (no Horizon hires; no Horizon revenue before the third quarter of 2027) to the December meeting.

## 5. Horizon

- FL: the market is moving. Claros Flow announced an NHS pilot on 14 October and is quoting "94% accuracy". Three prospects have raised it.
- DR summarised the prototype: seven-day forecasts for one hospital, back-tested over twelve months; typical error on occupied beds of two to three per cent. A shadow trial with 27 Kingsmere staff ends on 30 October.
- The Chair asked how the error compares with what the hospital can do without us. DR: that comparison is in the back-test files but has not been written up.
- The independent director asked what happens on the days the forecast is wrong, and who is accountable. No answer was recorded. **Action: leadership group.**
- TK asked whether Horizon is a medical device. DR and the CEO believed not. Regulatory analyst's view (reported by FL): it depends on the intended purpose we write down, and nobody has written it down.
- Kingsmere: the trust's Deputy Chief Operating Officer wants a pilot this winter. The data-sharing agreement covers discovery only. The trust's Chief Information Officer has not committed integration time. A paid pilot at £60k is in the pipeline for 18 December.
- **Agreed:** the board wants a named pilot customer this quarter. The Chair added that the board wants a pilot it can stand behind, and asked the leadership group to say plainly if those two things cannot both be had by December.

## 6. Funding

- TK: Northlight would consider leading a bridge of up to £1.5m in the first quarter of 2027. Two conditions were indicated, neither yet in writing: (a) a signed, paid Horizon pilot with an NHS trust by 31 December 2026; (b) a credible route to a second reference site by 31 March 2027.
- TK added that a bridge buys roughly six months at planned spending and that Northlight would want to see independent evidence of benefit before a Series B.
- The Chair asked that the leadership group's recommendation set out what each option costs and when the money runs out under each.

## 7. International

- FL reported interest from Kisima County (Kenya), with a possible grant from the Afya Bora Foundation of USD 150,000 over two years, and from RSUD Sukamaju (Indonesia) through the hospital-system vendor PT Nusa Medika Digital.
- The independent director asked whether either is a market or a favour. CW: neither produces meaningful revenue on current terms. FL: both produce a story about global reach that investors like.
- The Chair: "Explore" means find out what is true there, not start building. No engineering time to be committed before the December decision.
- **Agreed:** leadership group to include Kenya and Indonesia in its recommendation: what would have to be different, what it would cost, and what we would owe the people there if we started and stopped.

**Next meeting: 15 December 2026. Horizon recommendation is the main item.**
